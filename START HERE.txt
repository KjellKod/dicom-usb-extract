DICOM USB Extract

1. Insert the medical imaging USB drive.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. Choose the USB drive, the IMAGES folder, or a copied study folder.
4. Preview the images in the page.
5. Download one image, selected files, or everything as a ZIP.

Nothing is uploaded. Nothing from the USB drive is run.

There is no Mac app to approve, no Rosetta prompt, and no notarization step.
